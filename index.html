Ambient Nature

Privacy Policy · Effective August 24, 2026

Overview

Ambient Nature presents time, weather, sunlight, moon, and tide information through ambient scenes and iOS widgets. We designed the app to work without accounts, advertising, or behavioral tracking.

Location

With your permission, Ambient Nature uses your approximate current location to identify a nearby place, calculate local conditions, and find supported coastal tide stations. You may deny location access and use the included places instead. Location and selected-place information may be stored locally on your device and shared locally with the app’s widget.

Weather and tide services

To provide live information, the app sends coordinates for the selected place to:

  • Open-Meteo for weather and forecast data.
  • NOAA for supported U.S. coastal tide-station information and predictions.

Those providers may process network information according to their own policies. Ambient Nature does not control their systems.

Data collection

Ambient Nature does not create user accounts, sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or use third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. The app does not operate a developer server that stores your location or usage history.

Retention and control

App preferences and the current widget state remain on your device. You can revoke location access in iOS Settings and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

Children

Ambient Nature is a general-audience informational app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the beta evolves. Questions can be submitted through the Ambient Nature privacy support page.
